# Phase 35 AI Capabilities

## Smart search intents

Low stock, today's sales, unpaid invoices, best sellers, inactive customers, monthly profit, top supplier, dead stock, fast-moving items, and ERP help.

## Analysis

- Sales trends, revenue, cost, gross profit, and average invoice.
- Low stock, dead stock, fast movers, demand cover, and advisory reorder suggestions.
- Non-official revenue, expenses, gross/net profit summaries without posting.
- Purchase, customer behavior, and supplier ranking summaries.

## Knowledge and chat

The local knowledge center explains Dashboard, Reports, errors, Products, Backup, and Training. Chat uses the loaded snapshot and rejects execution-oriented language including execute, delete, insert, update, post, save, deploy, restore, activate, and suspend.

All output is advisory and read-only.
